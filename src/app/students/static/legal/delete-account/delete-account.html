01

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

Deleting your MBBS.NET account is a permanent, non-reversible action. Please review what is purged from our active systems:

Permanently Purged

Personal & Academic Data

  • Student profile, full name, WhatsApp number, email, date of birth
  • All mock test attempts, scores, and accuracy percentages
  • NEET, UCAT, GAMSAT, MCAT, and ISAT preparation history
  • Chapter mastery analytics, strengths, and weak-area reports
  • University invitation letters, bookmarks, and in-app chat history
  • AI tutor interaction history and personalized recommendations
Minimal Retention

Statutory Legal Records

  • Anonymized audit timestamps for fraud prevention & DPDP compliance
  • Encrypted transaction reference numbers (if financial transactions occurred)
  • All retained records are stripped of personal identifiers in cold storage

Important Warning: Once the deletion process completes, your test analytics, ranking badges, and saved university opportunities cannot be recovered.

Method C: In-App Deletion (iOS & Android)

If you have the MBBS.NET mobile application installed on your phone, you can initiate deletion natively:

Step 1

Open Profile

Launch the MBBS.NET app and tap the Profile tab in the bottom bar.

Step 2

Go to Settings

Tap the Settings gear icon and choose Account & Security.

Step 3

Delete Account

Scroll to the bottom and select Delete Account & Data.

Step 4

Verify OTP

Enter your WhatsApp verification code to authorize permanent removal.

05

Deletion Timeline & 7-Day Grace Period

Hour 0: Instant Deactivation

All active sessions are terminated, your profile is hidden from universities, and public access is disabled immediately.

Days 1 – 7: Grace Period

If you change your mind, contact support@mbbs.net to cancel the request and restore your full account history.

Day 7: Permanent Cryptographic Purge

All personal identifiers, test logs, and score records are wiped from active database clusters with zero recovery possibility.
