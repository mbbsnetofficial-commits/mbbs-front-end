1. Who We Are

{{ operator }} operates the MBBS.net platform ({{ platformUrl }}). MBBS.net is an education technology platform designed to help students explore medical education opportunities, prepare for entrance examinations, discover universities, receive university recommendations, interact with participating institutions, and use technology-enabled educational services.

Our Services include:

  • Medical entrance examination preparation
  • Practice questions and mock tests
  • Previous-year question resources
  • Learning and performance reports
  • University discovery & recommendation services
  • Student profiles & University invitations
  • Student-university communication
  • Notifications and reminders
  • AI-powered educational assistance
  • Other education-related technology services
02

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve our Services.

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you create an account or use our Services, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Date of birth or age where required
  • Country of residence & preferred country of study
  • Password or authentication credentials
  • Profile information & profile photograph (optional)
  • Communication and account preferences
  • Information provided during customer-support interactions
03

3. Educational and Academic Information

When you use our education and university-related Services, we may collect:

  • School or college information
  • Educational qualifications & academic performance
  • Examination scores & entrance examination details
  • Subjects studied & course preferences
  • Intended study destination & university preferences
  • English-language examination information
  • Eligibility and application-related information
  • Other voluntary educational information

This information is used strictly to provide personalized educational features, tailored practice tracks, and university recommendations.

04

4. Examination and Learning Data

When using examination preparation features, we collect:

  • Tests started and completed
  • Questions attempted & answers submitted (correct and incorrect)
  • Scores & time spent on questions/tests
  • Topic performance, test history, and learning progress
  • Mock examination results & previous-year question activity
  • Performance analytics

Used to generate test results, performance reports, topic analysis, personalized recommendations, and platform improvements.

05

5. University Finder and Recommendation Information

If you use the university discovery and recommendation service, we collect:

  • Academic qualifications, examination information, preferred countries, course preferences, English proficiency, and eligibility criteria.

Informational Notice

University recommendations are informational only and do not constitute a guarantee of admission, eligibility, scholarship, visa approval, licensing, or employment.

06

6. Student Profiles and University Invitations

Where you use features enabling participating institutions to discover or communicate with you, we may process:

  • Name, contact details, academic info, exam scores, course and university preferences.
  • Relevant profile information shared with participating universities.

Admissions Decision Notice

University invitations do not constitute guaranteed admission; all formal admission decisions remain solely with the relevant institution.

07

7. Chat and Communication Data

When using in-app messaging or communication features between users or universities, we process:

  • Messages, conversations, sender/recipient information, timestamps, delivery and read status, and account identifiers.

Prohibited Content Policy: Users must not send unlawful, abusive, threatening, fraudulent, harassing, defamatory, or discriminatory messages.

08

8. AI and Chatbot Features

When using AI features (such as educational chatbots, question explanations, and revision summaries), we process:

  • Prompts, questions, conversation logs, and educational context.
  • AI services may be supported by trusted third-party technology providers.

Caution: Do not submit sensitive personal data (such as passwords, payment cards, or government IDs) into the AI assistant. AI answers are provided for study support and should not replace professional medical, legal, immigration, or financial advice.

09

9. Device and Technical Information

We automatically collect technical telemetry when you access the platform:

  • IP address, device type and model, operating system, app version, browser type, device identifiers, network information, language preference, time zone, crash logs, diagnostic logs, and security audit data.
10

10. Location Information

Precise GPS location is not required for ordinary use unless explicitly requested with your permission.

General approximate location (such as country or city) is derived from IP addresses or voluntarily provided profile data to show region-relevant entrance exam structures and currency defaults.

11

11. Push Notifications

Notifications are sent for account activity, test reminders, learning alerts, university invitations, messages, and platform updates via Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). You can toggle these preferences in your device settings.

12

12. OTP, SMS, Email and WhatsApp Communications

Third-party communication providers are used to deliver one-time verification passwords (OTPs), university invite alerts, and critical account security messages (including authorized WhatsApp Business API providers and SMS gateways).

13

13. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies, local storage, SDKs, and session logs are used to keep you securely signed in, remember exam interface preferences, maintain application security, prevent fraud, and analyze platform performance.

14

14. How We Use Personal Information

We process personal data for legitimate educational and operational purposes:

  • Providing Services: Account authentication, test processing, results generation, analytics, university matches, communication, and customer support.
  • Personalization: Tailoring learning materials and recommendations.
  • Security: Account protection, fraud detection, infrastructure security, and terms enforcement.
  • Communications: OTPs, test reminders, system notifications, support replies.
  • Product Improvement: Diagnostic debugging, feature optimization, content updates.
  • Legal Compliance: Complying with applicable laws, resolving disputes, and enforcing legal rights.
15

15. How We Share Personal Information

Zero Standalone Sale: We do not sell student personal information under any circumstances.

Information is only shared under the following conditions:

  • Participating Universities: Shared only for requested educational opportunities and admissions inquiries you have engaged with.
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting, databases, authentication, AI, notifications, messaging (SMS/WhatsApp/Email), payment processing, analytics, and security.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by valid legal process, court orders, or government authorities.
  • Corporate Transactions: In the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or asset sale with equivalent data safeguards.
16

16. International Data Processing

Data may be processed or stored in secure server infrastructure outside your home country (such as cloud regions supporting high-availability services) with appropriate legal safeguards and encryption in transit.

17

17. Data Security

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information:

TLS 1.3 Encryption

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is strictly encrypted.

Hardware Keystore Tokens

Cryptographic tokens protect authentication sessions and API requests.

Role-Based Access Control

Strict access controls limit production database visibility to authorized personnel.

Continuous Auditing

Automated telemetry, vulnerability scanning, and daily offsite backups.

18. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Data is retained only as long as necessary for educational records, service provision, and legal compliance.

Right to Permanent Account Deletion

You can request permanent account deletion at any time. Account deletion removes access to test histories, recommendations, and student profiles.

View Deletion Procedure & Form or email support@mbbs.net

19. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Right to access and review information held about you.
  • Right to request correction or updating of inaccurate data.
  • Right to request erasure and permanent account deletion.
  • Right to withdraw consent previously provided for optional features.
  • Right to file grievances with our Grievance Officer or relevant data protection authorities.
