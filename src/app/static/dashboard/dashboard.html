Guidance for future medical students

LETTER TO FUTURE DOCTORS

Dear Medical Aspirant,

Choose your MBBS pathway carefully. Confirm that your university is recognised by the relevant medical authorities and offers strong academic teaching, clinical exposure and student support.

For Indian students, NEET qualification and current eligibility rules are essential. Review tuition, living costs, language requirements, hospital training and licensing pathways before applying.

A medical degree is a long-term commitment. Make your decision using verified information—not promises—and keep every admission document safely recorded.

With guidance,
